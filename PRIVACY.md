# Privacy Policy - My Bluetooth

Last updated: October 2026

My Bluetooth ("the app") shows your connected Bluetooth devices, their battery level, and lets you adjust sound settings on your phone.

## Data we collect
The app does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal data. The app has no account, no login, and no ads.

## Permissions and why they are used
- Bluetooth (nearby devices): to show the name and battery level of your connected Bluetooth devices.
- Display over other apps: to show the connection popup when a device connects.
- Notifications and foreground service: to keep the connection popup working in the background.
- Modify system settings: to change the left/right audio balance.
- Modify audio settings: to change volume, bass and equalizer.
- Start at boot: to restart the connection watcher after the phone restarts.

All information used by these features stays on your device. The only data saved is your app settings (for example, whether the popup is on), stored locally on your phone.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
